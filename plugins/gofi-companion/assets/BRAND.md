# GoFi Companion identity

A vinyl groove represents music fidelity. A download arrow and check represent saved, verified files. Warm ivory, deep ink and teal distinguish the brand without borrowing Spotify, Deezer or Apple marks. Light/dark logos share identical geometry; compact icons remove inner grooves. The generated cover is supplemental artwork, not an app screenshot. Raster icons are rendered from self-contained SVG source with no remote fonts or resources.
